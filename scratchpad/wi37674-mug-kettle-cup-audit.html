00 Where it stands

The retirement is real and already load-bearing — the kill switch exists, defaults to OFF, and eleven files enforce it. What remains is mostly residue: code that no longer runs, UI that no longer mounts, and prose that still describes the tier as live.

delivered  The switch and its enforcement

FLAGS.MUG_KETTLE_SYSTEM = "papercusp-mug-kettle-system", registered in DARK_FLAGS as case:'cutover'. Its polarity is inverted against every other dark flag: OFF is the delivered end state, ON is the reversible testing escape hatch. It therefore does not graduate by being flipped on — it graduates by being deleted, once the separation work below lands.

Eleven files consult the gate today:

  • packages/operator-core/lib/pot/retired-tier-roles.ts — the one definition of which roles are retired (mug, kettle, cup), alias-canonicalised
  • packages/operator-core/lib/agent-tools/_mug-kettle-gate.ts — the one refusal shape
  • …/agent-tools/cup/spawn.ts · overwatch/start.ts · overwatch/declare_wake.ts · pot/start.ts · pot/wake.ts — the 5 gated actuator tools
  • …/fleet/operator-spawn.ts · endpoint-route/routes/harness/spawn.ts · endpoint-route/routes/agent-mcp/bootstrap-role.ts — the 3 spawn doors
  • …/pot/started.ts · pot/watchdog.ts · pot/placement-watchdog.ts · scout/nudge-recipient.ts — the engine and the Scout seam

The ratchet guard (scripts/check-ungated-mug-kettle.mjs) reports 0 new ungated entry points against a 148-entry baseline, and 52 newly-gated ones waiting to be folded into that baseline. The guard is holding.

delivered  The Kettle's launcher is disarmed

All five system:overwatch-launch routine rows read active = false with next_fire_at = null (installs: papercusp-workspace, papercusp, gymloopharness, *, papercup). All thirteen system:cross-hive-outbox-drain rows are inactive too.

02 Role doors — CORRECTED 2026-08-10

⚠ Retraction — this section overstated the risk

It originally ran under the heading “Role doors carrying no gate of their own” and framed three files as gaps, one of them as a “real reference”. All three are UPSTREAM OF A GATE and none is a hole. I found this while working P-054: the ratchet's own roleDoorExemptions already documented it, with reasons — and I then verified each claim against the code rather than trusting the file:

  • converse.ts:692–699 calls spawnAgentInHarness, and the gate is ON THAT FUNCTION — fleet/operator-spawn.ts:886–897, isRetiredTierRole + mugKettleSystemEnabled, refusing with a message naming the flag.
  • durable-spawn.ts dispatches to /invoke — the gated spawn chokepoint.
  • psu-launcher.mjs posts to bootstrap-role — the gated console chokepoint.

What survives the correction: converse.ts still carries a real behavioural residue — the concierge's delegate path (L639–643, role === 'papercup') still routes buildable work to a Mug that will now refuse it. That is worth fixing, and is tracked as P-052 (now held by peer su-bdf16 alongside WI-37616, the nudgeMug callee layer). But it is a dead-end handoff, not an ungated spawn path.

What this cost: P-053 was authored off the wrong reading and has been dropped — adding a second gate behind three already-gated chokepoints is duplicate surface, not defence in depth. The lesson is the one this page preaches elsewhere and I still tripped on: the guard reported “3 role doors ungated”, and I read a guard's bookkeeping category as a security finding without opening the exemption list sitting in the same file.

net position  Six role-parameterized doors, zero ungated. Three carry the gate directly; three are exempt-with-reason because they funnel into one of those three. The ratchet records the exemptions so the next reader does not repeat my mistake.

10 Deliberately kept — do not sweep these up

The most likely way this retirement causes damage is over-reach. Four populations look like the tier and are not.

keep intact  Scout + Blender

Your directive was explicit that these survive whole. They are one subsystem, not two — there is no lib/blender/; the grading half lives in agent-tools/scout/grade-idea.ts and ideation-feedback.ts. Combined: lib/scout/ 84 files / 27,021 LOC + agent-tools/scout/ 6 files / 1,725 LOC.

keep intact  The shared pot substrate

50 of the 246 census entry points are marked substrate. loop:arm and loop:checkpoint — the su engine loop — import resolvePotHomeSlug from pot/wake. Gating this would break the replacement system with the very flag that retires the old one.

keep intact  Pot-as-container and P2P federation

pot:create, pot:list, pot:get, membership, moderation, beacon, takedown. A Pot is a harness container and a federation peer, not only a Mug host.

keep reachable  The stoppers

pot:pause and kettle:pause are deliberately not gated. Refusing a stop is never the safe direction — they must stay callable to wind the tier down if the flag is ever flipped ON and back OFF.

— What this audit cannot tell you

1 · The census reports PARTIAL coverage

Three blueprint-launch sites could not be resolved to a constant and are unclassified: agent-tools/pot/_create.ts:809, blueprint/launch-blueprint.ts:666 and :761. An empty category is only meaningful next to complete coverage.

2 · The census is not a complete spawn-door inventory

Every census detector keys on a role name appearing as a literal in source. Doors that take the role as data (body.role, searchParams.get('role')) are invisible to it by construction — which is exactly how the console door survived an earlier sweep. That is why the guard adds a second detector family anchored on the property (“can this turn a role name into a process?”) rather than the spelling. Do not cite the census alone as proof a door does not exist.

3 · Spelling is not membership, in both directions

hive, queen, bee and overwatch are pre-rename aliases still live in agent-tools/coordination/roles.ts, and real callers still use them (?role=queen was the production convention for Mug wakes). Meanwhile POT_BLUEPRINT_ID and OVERWATCH_BLUEPRINT_ID both evaluate to 'coding' — the same id an ordinary su session uses. A word-grep is wrong in both directions here; every number above comes from the mechanism-anchored detectors instead.

4 · Live rows are not in the source scan

Routine rows were queried separately against harness_shared.routines scoped to workspace_id = 'papercusp-workspace'. Other workspaces were not audited.
