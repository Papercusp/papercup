Conversations, at 384 pixels

The /adv Conversations surface is a three-column shell — nav rail │ list │ detail — over one deduplicated stream of Q&A, group decisions, agent chats and work-item mail. None of that geometry survives in the steering rail, which is 300–384px wide. This is the same data and the same write paths, re-laid-out as a single column that drills in instead of splitting. The pane below is live — filter it, open a row, reply, go back.

The problem

What has to give

/adv gives a conversation row 108px of height and five metadata fields laid out horizontally, plus a permanent detail pane beside it. At 384px there is room for roughly one of those things. Five decisions fall out of that.

1

The row loses its pane, not its content

Three lines — source · state · age, then the title over two lines, then who → whom · pot · work-item in mono. Roughly 68px against /adv's 108px, so about nine conversations are visible instead of five, and every field on the wide row survives.

2

Source becomes a colour, because it can't stay a word

/adv writes the source as a text pill — “Questions & discussions”, “Work-item mail”. That pill alone would eat a third of a narrow row, so source identity moves to a 2px left stripe plus a short caps label, and the four hues are read at a glance rather than parsed.

The hues are an analogous sweep off the app's own sky accent, deliberately avoiding emerald/amber/rose — those stay reserved for state, so a stripe can never be misread as a warning.

Q&A Decision Agent chat Mail state only state only
3

Facet filters collapse to four counted chips

/adv carries a six-column facet bar (type, state, sender, recipient, pot, related work). In the rail that becomes one search field and four source chips carrying live counts — enough to answer “anything new from the agents?”, which is what this pane is for. The full facet bar stays where the room is.

4

Detail replaces the list rather than sitting beside it

A back control, then the full detail: topics, body, accepted answer, linked work-item, every reply. Nothing is truncated away — it simply arrives one screen later. Each detail also carries an ↗ open in /adv escape for when the wide surface is the right tool.

5

You can still answer without leaving the rail

Reply and accept-as-answer on an open question, and Ask the agents from the footer — the same audited conversations/post, conversations/resolve and coord/ask calls /adv makes. Decisions, agent chats and mail stay read-only here, exactly as they are there.

Under it

One definition of a conversation, not two

The row-composition logic (composeUnifiedConversationRows, the system-actor filter, the dedup signature and the row types) currently lives inside the 4,209-line AdvConversationsTab.tsx. I'd lift it into a shared components/conversations/unified-conversations.ts that both surfaces import, so the two can never drift on what counts as a conversation or which rows are hidden as system telemetry.

  • Same four useSyncQuery reads — SSE-cached, so opening the tab hits a warm cache.
  • Queries gate on the tab being active; an unopened tab costs nothing.
  • Selection in the URL as ?lsc=threads:<id> — deep-linkable and agent-driveable, per CLAUDE.md.
  • Styles as a .pclsb-conv block in the rail's existing stylesheet. No new CSS file.
