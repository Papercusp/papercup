# Authoring a design acceptance contract

Policy version: desktop-rollout-2026-09-08.1. Governing decisions: design-acceptance-journey-enforcement-2026-09-08#D-001 and #D-002.

Record the approved image's immutable revision and SHA-256, its approval source, the implementing plan/items, and the actual destination route in the existing design-phase ratification. Copy its returned binding into each implementing work-item's `designReferences`. An exploratory image creates no obligation. A title containing “UI”, “screen”, or “desktop” creates no obligation either.

Pin `approval.acceptance` to the contract path, contract SHA-256 and evidence path. Declare every entry variant through the actual shell: clean navigation, canonical links, supported legacy links, persisted selection, reload and history. A mounted component or a passing unrelated item cannot discharge the navigation obligation.

Declare the required states, viewport sizes, themes, geometry and usable controls. Retain the approved image beside each actual capture, with a review bound to the capture hash and build identity. Keep fixture observations separate from real service observations. A placeholder or a locally simulated viewer must never be labeled a working remote desktop. Raster mockups carry structural and reviewed visual obligations; calibrated pixel thresholds apply only to commensurable captures.

Measure source, tests, fixtures, rubric, environment and served-build identity when producing evidence. The reader remeasures them before completion and shipment. Missing registration/proof, a wrong destination, stale identities, missing cases and invalid captures each remain a refusal. Repair the failed obligation and refresh its proof; do not replace the approved reference with a new screenshot of the drifting implementation.

## Historical references and explicit adoption

An existing reference without an approval contract keeps the existing host-calibration policy. Do not classify or migrate it by searching its title. Adoption requires a newly approved reference revision with explicit plan/item/route scope and current proof. An existing adopted obligation stays binding; deleting its approval or acceptance fields is refused.

The initial cohort is the exact option-B desktop canary in the Papercusp harness. Wider adoption must supply `approval.rollout` with a policy path, policy SHA-256 and independent review-card reference. The versioned policy lists exact harness/feature/plan/item/route cohorts, the original-negative/repaired-positive canary pair, every required negative control, this guidance and the historical policy. All evidence artifacts are byte-pinned.

The independent acceptance review uses the existing prevention-plan rubric. Every criterion must be healthy. Its `r-5` evidence must be a JSON object containing `designRolloutPolicySha256` equal to the reviewed policy hash, `verdict: "accepted"`, and `canaryGrade: "pass"`; additional explanation and evidence links may be JSON fields. Review must come from an eligible independent grader. A missing, provisional, retracted, mismatched or failed review does not authorize expansion. A changed policy needs a new review.

## Limited exceptions

An exception can defer only a named **new adoption**. Record an id and version, exact cohort, owner, reason, approval reference, start and expiry. Maximum duration is seven days. An active deferral refuses that new ratification; an expired exception requires a reviewed policy revision. No exception waives an existing failed proof, removes a binding, silently changes a reference, or establishes connectivity. Use an explicitly approved reference revision to record agreed design adaptations.

The initial recommendation names no wider cohorts and grants no exceptions. Review the desktop pilot first. Expanding a cohort is a subsequent versioned policy change, backed by its own current evidence and independent acceptance; this document does not itself authorize it.
