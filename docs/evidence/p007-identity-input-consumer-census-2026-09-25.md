Scope: the 24 `contributions[]` declared by the built-in `su*` identity documents in `libs/papercusp/packages/harness/blueprints/` on 2026-09-25, plus the eight `BUNDLE_KINDS` in `libs/papercusp/packages/orchestrator/src/blueprint/slots.ts`. The dynamic installed-identity space is covered by the schema and selected-source adapter tests, not by pretending its future members can be enumerated now. `C` means a named canonical test exercises the source/consumer path; `U` means the path still needs a detector or live proof. These are source-test statuses, not deployed acceptance.

The source reference in every row is the named identity's `blueprint.yaml` and its `contributions[]` entry. Fixed prompt refs resolve beside that source under `prompts/`; provider refs name the existing producer, never reusable live data. The compiler records `contribution.<id>` with `fixed:source-change`, `provider:<refresh>`, or an explicit `omitted:<reason>` provenance decision. Required omissions fail compilation; optional omissions require receipts. `role-launch-spec.test.ts` tests a valid selected provider fixture alongside both explicit and silent omission of its required generated setting; focused runs 19484550 and 19491714 passed. Copy-out mutation ledger row 19491734 changed that fixture's required setting to optional, failed the guarded test, and verified the original file byte-identical; baseline rows 19491732 and 19491733 passed. `agent-identities/source.test.ts` iterates every built-in SU manifest, checks that all 24 declarations have census rows naming source, refresh and a claimed consumer test, and verifies compiled provider provenance and omission behavior. Its committed population test passed on the original census and failed when a copy-out mutant omitted `su/current-mode-state` (23 rows against 24 declarations; WI-10002964 post 1146099). The row-name check is population accounting, not proof that each named consumer ran. Its root SU case exposed and now guards EI-24262164804137120: inherited `su.practice` declarations must be read from the resolved document, with both root SU mode and assignment supplied; it failed before the source fix (19491720) and the full suite passed after (19491728, 11/11).
