## Brief

**Modes** are standing instructions you switch on in conversation to change
how much an agent asks versus acts. **AUTO** = "act on your judgment, don't
ask, disclose after". **IDEATE** = "also invent new features, don't just
patch". **DRAIN** = "take this backlog and drive every item to done".
Say the word ("AUTO mode", "exit AUTO mode") and it holds for the whole
session.

## Details

Default (no mode): agents propose plans, confirm the approach, and check
before writing — right for high-stakes or unfamiliar work. **AUTO** flips
that: the agent decides, acts, and reports what it did with its assumptions
listed, instead of pausing to ask. It stays on until you turn it off. **COLD
AUTO** is AUTO carried across scheduled wakes — the agent keeps going on a
loop while you're away, same act-don't-ask grant, no human in the room.

**IDEATE** raises ambition rather than autonomy: the agent deliberately
brainstorms capabilities the system *should* have, files them as reviewable
proposals, and (combined with AUTO) builds the strongest ones. **DRAIN** is a
mission: hand over a work-item backlog and the agent — often leading a fleet
of helpers — triages, prioritizes, and drives every item to a terminal state,
reporting the residue that genuinely needs you.

Modes compose ("AUTO + IDEATE" = invent and build). They're also honest:
whatever an agent did under a mode is disclosed and recorded, so autonomy
never means invisibility.
